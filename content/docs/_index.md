## FAQ about organization and finances
Q: How does project development work?
A: Project development is carried out through:
- planning and discussion via project meetings
- plan the distribution of profits 
- executing the plan
- marketing and promotion
- distribute, enjoy, and store the profits.

Q: Knowledge sharing process?
A: The knowledge sharing involves:
- regular knowledge sharing meetings
- documentation writing

Q: How is the profit distributed?
A: The profit is distributed based on:
- collective discussion of the contributions made by each member via meetings
- a collective information voting system
- a mathematical formula agreed upon previously

Q: what about the expenses?
A: The expenses are handled through:
- collective discussion of the necessary costs via meetings

Q: How is the accumulated profit handled?
A: The accumulated profit is managed through:
- an information system that records the income or investment/donation transactions as (name, amount, date) tuples and recorded via `git commit`

Q: The design of voting system?
A: The voting system is designed not like one person one vote, but rather based on more complex data structures:
- design the list of eligible options
- each member gives their preferred ranking of the options
- an algorithm is used to aggregate the rankings and determine the final outcome, which is also a list of ranked options

Q: Onboarding process?
A: The onboarding process involves:
- joining the knowledge sharing meetings regularly
- contributing to projects regularly
- familiarizing oneself with every aspect of the organization and its projects
